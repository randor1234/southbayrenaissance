On an iPhone

  1. Download the sticker, then open it in Photos and tap Share, Copy Photo.
  2. Open your event photo, tap Edit, then Markup.
  3. Tap the plus, choose Add Text or paste the sticker, and drag it where you want it.

Instagram or LinkedIn stories

  1. Save the story overlay to your camera roll.
  2. Start a story with your photo or video.
  3. Add the overlay as a sticker from your photos and pinch it to fill the screen.

On video

Drop the story overlay on a track above your footage in CapCut, Premiere, Final Cut or iMovie. It is already transparent, so no keying or masking needed.
